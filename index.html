Who am I?

A Lead Full Stack Developer based in Durham Region, Ontario

I'm a Lead Full Stack Developer with over nine years of experience building web and mobile applications, from a near real-time data collection system at E-Data Now! to leading the frontend and backend teams behind YHP's Wonderlab event platform. In 2020 I founded Johnston Software Development Inc., where I design, build and modernize web and mobile apps for clients, most recently an ongoing platform modernization for a luxury watch subscription club. I work across the stack with Vue and Nuxt, React and Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript and Flutter, and I enjoy mentoring developers and leading teams.
